Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using Zuste for your business.

How does Zuste work?

expand_more

Zuste connects brands with authentic creators. You can browse portfolios, request collaborations, and manage the entire booking and payment process securely within our platform.

How do I book a creator?

expand_more

Once you find a creator you love, simply click 'Request Booking' on their profile. Fill out your campaign details, and they will respond within 48 hours to confirm availability and rates.

What payment methods are accepted?

expand_more

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal, and direct bank transfers for larger enterprise accounts. All payments are securely processed.

Can I cancel a booking?

expand_more

Yes, you can cancel a booking up to 7 days before the agreed content delivery date for a full refund. Cancellations made within 7 days may be subject to a partial fee depending on the creator's policy.

Is my payment secure?

expand_more

Absolutely. We use industry-standard encryption to protect your financial information. Funds are held in escrow and only released to the creator once you approve the final content.
